Meta ads services

What does a Meta ads agency do for you?

The short answer

A Meta ads agency plans, builds and runs your Facebook and Instagram ads, then reports what each ringgit brought back. At ADV that covers campaign management, WhatsApp and lead form campaigns, creative direction and testing, audiences and retargeting, pixel and Conversions API tracking, and a weekly report. You pay Meta for the ads and pay us to run them.

01

Facebook and Instagram ads management

Campaign structure, budgets, bidding and placements across Feed, Stories and Reels, all from one Meta ad account.

02

Click-to-WhatsApp ads

Ads that open a WhatsApp chat with your team. Our home energy services client gets its Meta leads this way.

03

Lead form campaigns

Instant forms inside Facebook and Instagram, with a question or two that filters out people who are only curious.

04

Creative direction and testing

We write the copy, brief the visuals and test hooks against each other. Production is quoted separately.

05

Audiences and retargeting

Broad and interest targeting to find new people, plus retargeting for those who visited, watched or messaged.

06

Pixel and Conversions API tracking

We set up the Meta pixel and Conversions API so Meta learns from real leads and sales, not clicks.

07

Weekly reporting

Spend, leads, cost per lead and what we changed, every week, in plain language.

08

Your account stays yours

The ad account, page, pixel and audiences sit under your business. We work inside them with the access you give us.

Read the detail

Boosting a post is not the same as running a campaign. A boost is a quick way to buy reach for one post, with few controls over who sees it or what Meta optimises for. A campaign built in Ads Manager lets you choose the goal, like a lead, a WhatsApp chat or a purchase. Meta then looks for people likely to take that action.

WhatsApp or lead forms? It depends on how your team sells. WhatsApp ads suit businesses that close in conversation, like home services, clinics and education. People message you straight away, so how fast you reply matters. Lead forms suit businesses that call back, like B2B services, property and insurance. Forms are easy to fill in, so we add a question or two to slow down people who are not serious.

Creative. On Meta, the ad itself does a lot of the targeting. The first line, or the first three seconds of a video, decides who stops scrolling. We write the ad copy, brief the images and videos, and run tests so you know which angle brings in the cheapest qualified leads. If you need photos or video shot, that production is quoted separately, or your own team can supply the material.

Tracking. Meta learns from the results you report back to it. If the pixel only sees page views, Meta finds people who view pages. We set up the pixel and the Conversions API, which sends events from your website server or CRM, so Meta can learn from real leads and sales. It is also the first thing we check in an audit.

How we work

How does ADV run your Facebook and Instagram ads?

Four steps, in this order. Tracking comes before any new campaign, because Meta can only learn from what it can see.

  1. 01

    Free audit

    View-only access to your Meta Business Manager. We show you what works, what does not and what we would change first.

  2. 02

    Tracking first

    Pixel, Conversions API and the right conversion events, checked against the leads your team actually received.

  3. 03

    Rebuild

    Fewer, cleaner campaigns and ad sets, each with enough budget to learn, plus new creative to test.

  4. 04

    Weekly optimisation

    Each week we cut what is not working, move budget to what is and add new creative. You get a report every week.

Taking over an account that is already running? Our guide on how we fix Facebook ad accounts walks through each step in detail.

Read the detail

The audit. Every engagement starts with a free ad account audit. You give us view-only access through Meta Business Manager, so nothing changes while we look. We check the account structure, tracking, audiences, creative and the cost per lead trend. You keep the findings whether or not you hire us.

Tracking first. We confirm which events fire, whether each lead is counted once, and whether the numbers in Ads Manager match the enquiries your team received. If WhatsApp is your main channel, we make sure conversations are counted as results. Clients own their ad accounts, and we mainly set up this tracking for them.

The rebuild. Many accounts we see have too many ad sets, each with too little budget to learn. Meta's Business Help Centre says an ad set usually needs about 50 optimisation events in a week to exit the learning phase. So on smaller budgets we run fewer ad sets and give each one enough to work with.

Weekly optimisation. We do not make big changes every day, because significant edits can send an ad set back into learning. Once a week we review results, pause the weakest ads, shift budget and add new creative to test. You hear from Alvin directly, and replies come within 2 business hours.

An agency brings experience from many ad accounts. Ask who will actually run your account, and make sure the ad account is in your name, not theirs.

A freelancer is usually the cheapest and the most direct. That suits a clear, contained job, like setting up tracking or launching one campaign. The risk is cover. If they get busy, fall sick or take a full-time job, the work stops.

Fit

Who is a Meta ads agency a good fit for, and who is it not?

Meta ads work best when people will act once they see what you sell. They work less well when nobody is looking for it yet and the budget is very small.

A good fit

You are likely a good fit if

  • You sell something people decide on after seeing it, like home services, beauty, education, F&B or B2B services.
  • You can spend about RM1,500 to RM2,000 a month on ads for lead generation.
  • Someone on your team replies to WhatsApp messages and leads quickly.
  • You want to own your ad account and see the numbers every week.
  • You can commit to at least 3 months.
Not yet

We are probably not right if

  • Your ad budget is under RM1,500 a month and cannot grow.
  • People search for what you sell when they urgently need it. Google Search may suit you better first.
  • You need TV, outdoor and media buying under one contract.
  • You want results guaranteed by a fixed date.
  • Your product falls under Meta's restricted or prohibited ad categories.
Read the detail

Industries. On Meta we have worked with home energy services, B2B services, beauty and wellness, and F&B, among others. The common thread is a clear offer and a team that follows up fast.

Search first, or Meta first? If people already type what you sell into Google, like an urgent repair, Google Search often brings ready buyers sooner. Meta is better at reaching people who were not looking yet but will act on a good offer. Some of our clients run both. See our Google Ads agency service if Search is your gap.

Do you need a website? It depends on the industry. WhatsApp ads and lead forms can work without one. Online stores, and businesses where buyers want to check you out first, usually need a clear landing page.
