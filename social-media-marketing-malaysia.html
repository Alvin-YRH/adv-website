The platforms

Which social media platforms matter in Malaysia in 2026?

The short answer

For most Malaysian businesses, the platforms that matter are Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and WhatsApp. Malaysia had 30.7 million active social media user identities in October 2025, 85.0% of the population (DataReportal, Digital 2026: Malaysia). You do not need all of them. Pick the one or two where your buyers are, and use WhatsApp to talk to the people who reply.

DataReportal counted 35.4 million internet users in Malaysia in October 2025, 98% of the population, and 30.7 million active social media user identities. It advises caution when comparing that figure with earlier editions, because the counting method changed.

People spread their time across many apps. Malaysians used an average of 8.12 social platforms each month, above the global average of 6.83 (DataReportal, Digital 2025 Global Overview). That is why "be on every platform" sounds right and rarely works for a small team.

Money follows the attention. Social media took 41% of total Malaysian ad spend in 2024, ahead of search at 24% (MAGNA, reported by Bernama). In a Vase.ai survey of 417 Malaysian consumers in September 2025, 35% said they bought a product after seeing it on social media.

Audience and language

Which platform fits your audience and language?

Start from who you sell to and the language they use. In our accounts, Instagram works well for Chinese-speaking lead generation, and TikTok works for Malay content.

Read the detail

Malaysia is not one audience. The same need is talked about in English, Malay and Chinese, and each group has its own habits. One English ad shown to everyone usually pays for reach that does not reply.

Chinese-speaking buyers. Write the ad in Chinese from the start. A translated English ad reads like a translation, and people notice.

Malay-speaking buyers. On TikTok, the content itself should be in Malay: a person speaking on camera the way your buyers talk, not an English video with Malay captions.

Mixed audiences. Facebook and Instagram run from the same Meta ad account. Split ads by language, then let the results tell you where to put more budget.

Organic and paid

Organic or paid social: what is each one for?

Organic posts build trust with people who already know you. Paid ads bring in leads predictably. Most businesses need both, but the leads usually come from paid.

Organic vs paid social

Read the detail

Organic social is what you post for free. Paid social is ads. Most disappointment comes from expecting one to do the other's job.

Organic builds trust. Recent posts, real photos and replies to comments tell a new buyer you are real and paying attention. That matters most when someone is deciding whether to message you. It is slow, and reach goes mainly to people who already follow you.

Paid brings leads predictably. You choose who sees the ad and what they do next, such as send a WhatsApp message. You can see what each lead costs and adjust week by week, so it is the part you can plan a budget around.

Together. Your best organic posts make good ads, because people already showed they care. If you only have time for one thing, put a steady ad budget behind one clear offer and keep the page tidy.

Content

What should you post, and how often?

Post what answers your buyer's questions, on a rhythm you can keep for months. Keeping going matters more than posting a lot.

01

Answers to real questions

The questions people ask on WhatsApp every week: price, timing, what is included.

02

Proof

Finished jobs, customer feedback you have permission to share, and results with dates.

03

The people behind it

Who does the work and how. People buy from people.

04

Short video

Vertical, with the point in the first seconds and captions on. It works on TikTok, Reels and as ads.

05

Festive and sale periods

Ramadan and Hari Raya, Chinese New Year, Deepavali, 11.11 and 12.12. Plan these weeks ahead.

06

One clear offer

A reason to message now: a free check, a quote, a trial. Say what happens after they message.

Pick a posting rhythm you can hold for three months, then keep it. A plan that stops in week three does more harm than a smaller one that keeps going.

Read the detail

There is no number of posts a week that works for every business, and we will not invent one. Pages that post steadily about what buyers ask give ads a better place to land. Pages that post a lot for a month and then stop look abandoned.

Start with your inbox. Write down the ten questions people ask most on WhatsApp and answer one per post. These posts also make good ads, because they answer the doubt that stops people from messaging.

Keep video simple. Video took 22% to 24% of Malaysian digital ad spend in the first half of 2025 (MSA, MAA and MDA). A clear phone video of the person who does the work, in your buyers' language, is often enough.

Check the rules. Some industries have rules beyond platform policy. Health service ads in Malaysia need approval from the Medicine Advertisements Board (KKLIU) before they run, under Act 290.

From social to leads

How do you turn social media into leads?

Run ads that start a conversation, make replying easy, and come back to the people who did not message the first time.

  1. Click to WhatsApp ads. One tap opens a chat with a message already typed. Best for most service businesses here.
  2. Instant lead forms. The form opens inside Facebook or Instagram, already filled with the person's details. Good for B2B and callbacks.
  3. Retargeting. Show a second ad to people who watched your video, visited your page or opened the chat but did not finish.

Read the detail

Likes do not pay the bills. The goal is a conversation with someone who might buy, at a cost that works for your business.

Click to WhatsApp ads. The person taps the ad, WhatsApp opens with a message already written, and they press send. No form, no page to load. For our home energy services client, the best campaign ran at RM10.25 per lead with a 3.2% click through rate.

Instant lead forms. When you need details before you call, a Meta lead form works well. Add one or two questions that filter out people who are not a fit.

Retargeting. Most people do not message the first time. A second, more direct ad to people who watched a video or opened a chat is usually a small part of the budget.

Tracking. You need to see which ad brought which lead. Our clients own their ad accounts, and the main thing we set up for them is the tracking, so the budget goes to ads that bring customers, not just clicks.

Results should improve over time. A beauty and wellness client's Meta cost per lead went from RM22 to RM14.04 over three months, 36% lower, on RM1,500 to RM1,800 a month.

Ad spend. This goes to Meta or TikTok, not the agency. For Meta lead generation, about RM1,500 to RM2,000 a month is the practical floor in our experience. Choosing help

How do you choose a social media agency in Malaysia?

Decide first whether you need posting or leads. Then ask who owns the ad account, what gets reported and whether the results have dates.

01Fit

Content agency or ads agency?

A content agency plans and posts. A performance agency runs ads for leads. Many businesses need the second and hire the first.

The ad account, page and pixel should sit in your business. If you leave, your data stays with you.
