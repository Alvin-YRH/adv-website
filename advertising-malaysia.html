The short answer

For ads in Malaysia, a Meta lead conversation costs about RM10 to RM22 in our recent accounts, and above RM40 in harder verticals such as beauty, and a Google Search lead costs from RM2.41 in high-volume insurance to RM59.12 in specialist healthcare. Meta awareness CPMs run about RM1.17 to RM1.41. These figures come from our own digital advertising accounts, past and current, not from published averages. Current client figures are for the 30 days to 18 Nov 2026.

RM1,500 to RM2,000 a month is our practical floor for Meta lead generation. Below it, results come too slowly to judge. In our accounts, Google Search needs around RM3,000 a month. Multi-channel becomes viable around RM5,000. Agency management fees sit on top of ad spend. Management fees start from RM800 a month, depending on objective and account size. For Malaysian business accounts, 8% SST applies to ad spend.

Which to start with: Google Search if people already search for what you sell; Meta if your product needs to be shown before anyone thinks to search for it. Under RM3,000/month, run one channel properly rather than two badly.

Choosing a channel

Should you start with
Google or Meta?

Intent decides, not preference. If people already search for what you sell, start on Google. If they need to see it first, start on Meta.

Does anyone search for what you sell? If a person with your problem types it into Google, like "aircond service kl", "roof leak repair kl" or "invisalign price", then Google Search is where the money is. You're capturing demand that already exists, at the moment it exists. Higher cost per click, far higher intent.

Or does your product need to be seen first? Nobody searches for a skincare brand they've never heard of, or a restaurant that opened last week. Meta creates the demand Google later captures. Lower cost per impression, more work to convert.

Under RM3,000/month, pick one. The most common mistake we see in Malaysian accounts is a RM2,000 budget split across Meta and Google, leaving both too thin to produce results you can judge. One channel at RM2,000 beats two at RM1,000 almost every time.

Then measure honestly. Channel choice matters far less than measurement quality: an account with broken conversion tracking wastes budget identically on every platform. That's the first thing we fix in Meta takeovers and Google Ads takeovers alike.

Read the detail

Lead generation. This is most of what we run: clinics, home services, insurance, B2B. If people already type your service into Google, Search catches them at the moment they need you. If they do not, Meta creates the demand and sends the lead to WhatsApp. In our accounts, Instagram works well for Chinese-speaking audiences in lead generation, so we plan placements and copy around who you sell to.

E-commerce. Here the website is the shop. If checkout is slow or purchase tracking is broken, no channel will fix it, so we check tracking before we spend. Google Search and Performance Max pick up people who are ready to buy. Meta and TikTok bring in people who have not heard of you yet.

Local business. Restaurants, salons and shops need people nearby to know they exist. Meta awareness on a small radius is cheap reach. Google Search covers people who look for your type of business in your area.

Awareness and launches. Meta gives the widest reach for the money. TikTok works for Malay content in the accounts we run. Measure these campaigns on reach and cost per view, then let Search or retargeting pick up the demand they create. Some people call this demand marketing: you create demand on Meta or TikTok, then capture it on Google.

The website question. Whether you need a full website depends on the industry. A home service business can do well with WhatsApp and a simple page. An online shop cannot.
