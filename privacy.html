In short: this website uses Google Analytics to understand visits. We only collect your name and contact details if you message us. We use client data only to run your campaigns, we never sell personal data, and you can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold at any time.

1. Who we are

ADV Digital Marketing (SSM Business Registration No. 202603036290 (AS0509730-W)) is a sole proprietorship owned by Yap Rong Hui (Alvin Yap), based in Bukit Jalil, 58200 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. We are the data user responsible for the personal data described in this notice. Person responsible for data protection: Alvin Yap — Alvin.Yap@advmarketing.biz.

2. What we collect

When you visit this website

We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are read, roughly where visitors are (city level), what device and browser they use, how they found us, and whether they click our WhatsApp or email buttons. This uses cookies (see section 9). Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, processes your IP address to deliver the site and block attacks. There are no forms on this website.

When you contact us

If you message us on WhatsApp, email us or call us, we receive your name, phone number or email address, your company name if you give it, and what you tell us about your business.

When you are a client

To run your marketing, we handle business contact details, access to your ad, analytics and website accounts, campaign performance data, and — where we manage lead handling for you — the enquiries your customers send. For those customer enquiries we act on your instructions as a data processor. We also keep billing records.

When you apply to work with us

We keep the CV and contact details you send so we can assess your application.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare proposals.
  • To deliver, measure and report on the services you engage us for.
  • To invoice and meet our tax and legal obligations.
  • To understand which pages help visitors, and improve the website.
  • To publish anonymised results — for example on our Live Results Board. We never name or identify a client, or any of their customers, without written permission.

We do not use your data for automated decisions that affect you, and we do not send you marketing messages unless you ask for them. You can tell us to stop at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, under their own data-protection terms:

  • Google — Workspace (email, documents), Analytics, and Google Ads when we manage your account.
  • Meta — WhatsApp, and Facebook and Instagram ads when we manage your account.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and security.
  • Notion — client and project records.
  • Make.com — workflow automation between these tools.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — AI assistance for analysis, reporting and drafting.
  • Reporting connectors (such as Windsor.ai) — to pull campaign data into reports.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

5. Transfers outside Malaysia

Several of these providers store data outside Malaysia, including in Singapore, the United States and the European Union. We use established providers whose data-protection standards are comparable to Malaysian law, as the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 requires.

6. How we protect it

Access to client accounts uses the minimum permission needed, accounts are protected with two-factor authentication where available, and access is removed when an engagement ends. If a data breach is likely to cause significant harm, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner and the people affected, as the 2024 amendments to the Act require.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't become engagements: up to 24 months.
  • Client and billing records: 7 years, as Malaysian tax law requires.
  • Access to your ad and analytics accounts: removed when the engagement ends.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months.
  • Google Analytics data: according to Google Analytics' retention settings, no more than 14 months.

8. Your rights

Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (as amended in 2024) you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct data that is wrong or out of date;
  • withdraw your consent, or ask us to stop processing for direct marketing;
  • ask for your data to be transferred to another organisation (data portability).

Email Alvin.Yap@advmarketing.biz or message us on WhatsApp. We reply within 21 days and don't charge for reasonable requests. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia.

9. Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_* to count visits; they contain no name or contact details. Cloudflare may set a security cookie (__cf_bm) that is needed for the site to work. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on — the site works the same without them.

10. Singapore

If you are in Singapore, we also handle your personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012, and you have the same rights to access and correct it. Contact us the same way.

11. Changes and language

We will update this notice when our practices change; the date at the top shows the latest version. This notice is published in English and Bahasa Melayu. If the two versions differ, the English version applies.
